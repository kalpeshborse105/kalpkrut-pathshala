# MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
# COACHING INSTITUTE MANAGEMENT ERP
## Complete Institute, Academy, Tuition Centre, Training Centre & Learning Management Software
### Laravel + PHP + MySQL | Production-Ready Enterprise Application

---

# 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Act as a senior Laravel architect, education technology consultant, ERP developer, database architect, UI/UX designer, and academic operations specialist.

Your task is to develop a complete, production-ready Coaching Institute Management ERP using Laravel, PHP, and MySQL.

This must be a comprehensive business management and academic management system, not merely a student registration application.

The application must manage the complete lifecycle of an educational institute:

Enquiry → Lead Follow-up → Counselling → Admission → Course Enrollment → Batch Allocation → Fee Collection → Attendance → Teaching → Assignments → Examinations → Results → Certificates → Placement → Alumni.

The system must support:

1. Coaching institutes.
2. Tuition centers.
3. Competitive examination coaching.
4. School tuition centers.
5. Computer training institutes.
6. Digital marketing academies.
7. Web development training centers.
8. Software development academies.
9. Vocational training centers.
10. Skill development centers.
11. Language training institutes.
12. Professional certification institutes.
13. Online learning academies.
14. Hybrid education centers.
15. Multi-branch education businesses.

The application should be configurable for different educational business models.

Primary market: India.

# 41. IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES

Implement these rules strictly.

## Admission

- Every admission must have a unique admission number.
- Every student must have a valid enrollment record.
- Course and branch must be valid.
- Admission history must be maintained.
- Duplicate student detection must be available.
- Admission cancellation must preserve financial history.

## Course and Batch

- Batch must belong to a valid course and branch.
- Students cannot be allocated beyond batch capacity without authorization.
- Faculty must be assigned to eligible subjects.
- Class schedules must not conflict.
- Historical batch allocation must be preserved.

## Attendance

- Attendance must be linked to a valid class session.
- Duplicate attendance must be prevented.
- Attendance corrections must be auditable.
- Attendance percentage must be calculated correctly.
- Only authorized roles can modify locked attendance.

## Fees

- Every student fee assignment must be linked to an admission or approved enrollment.
- Installments must have due dates.
- Partial payments must be supported.
- Payment allocations must not exceed the available unallocated amount.
- Refunds must reference original payments.
- Discounts must follow approval limits.
- Receipt numbers must be unique.

## Examinations

- Students must satisfy configured eligibility rules.
- Exam attempts must follow configured attempt limits.
- Answers must be saved reliably.
- Objective marks must be calculated consistently.
- Descriptive marks must support manual evaluation.
- Published results must not be silently modified.
- Result corrections must be auditable.

## Certificates

- Certificates must only be issued after configured completion requirements.
- Certificate IDs must be unique.
- Revoked certificates must remain verifiable as revoked.

## Finance

- All journal entries must balance.
- Posted entries must not be silently edited.
- Corrections must use reversals.
- Fee collection, refunds, scholarships, and expenses must be traceable.
